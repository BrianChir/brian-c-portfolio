problem: 'Retrospectives are the most valuable agile ceremony and the most frequently skipped. Teams suffer from dominant voices, shallow insights, and action items that never get followed up. Existing tools collect sticky notes and run votes but do not help facilitators see patterns, surface what is not being said, or track whether the team is actually improving.'
solution: 'Co-developed with Richard Magu. Designed the AI as a co-facilitator: it suggests discussion prompts based on previous retro data, synthesizes themes from team input in real time, identifies recurring patterns across retrospectives, and automates post-retro summaries and action item tracking. The human facilitator always controls the flow. Validated the concept through a presentation-first approach at PMI Kenya Chapter before committing to backend engineering.'
outcome: 'Live platform at yoda-ai.onrender.com. Presented at PMI Kenya Chapter with live Mentimeter polling that validated the co-facilitation concept with real practitioners. Beta testing active with feedback informing iteration priorities. Pre-retro prep automation, post-retro summaries within 1 hour, and weekly action item check-ins implemented.'
