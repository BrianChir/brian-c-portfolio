product: 'A personal finance Progressive Web App for East African users who transact primarily through M-PESA. It parses mobile money statements, auto-categorises transactions using an AI taxonomy built for East African money movement, and surfaces net worth across mobile money, bank accounts, and cash.'
problem: 'Over 70% of financial transactions in East Africa flow through M-PESA, but most personal finance tools are designed for Western banking: card transactions, direct debits, and subscription billing. East African users lack visibility into spending, savings growth, and net worth across the fragmented accounts they actually use.'
solution: 'Built an M-PESA statement parser with a 14-category AI-powered taxonomy trained on 500+ real transaction descriptions, including agent fees, person-to-person transfers, airtime top-ups, and till payments. Chose Progressive Web App architecture after user interviews showed 60% of target users avoid large app downloads because of data costs and storage limits, and made zero-plaintext encryption a non-negotiable security layer.'
definingDecision: 'Made the Net Worth Dashboard the anchor feature instead of budgeting tools after 12 Nairobi user interviews showed people were more motivated by watching wealth grow than by being told to restrict spending.'
outcome: 'The parser reached 94% transaction categorisation accuracy across the 14-category taxonomy. Zero-plaintext encryption became the platform security standard, and the migration from Lovable Cloud to Supabase + Vercel happened without service disruption while improving query performance and reducing hosting cost.'
