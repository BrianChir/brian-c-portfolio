problem: 'A solar water pump costs around KES 120,000, while many smallholder farmers earn roughly KES 800 on a good day and cannot access bank loans without payslips, collateral, or formal credit histories. The financing solution also had to work for farmers and field engineers using feature phones, not smartphone apps or reliable mobile data.'
solution: 'Designed the platform around the channels farmers and field engineers already used: M-PESA for repayments and USSD for service workflows. Prioritised offline-compatible field operations over app-based convenience, and made the M-PESA C2B reconciliation engine idempotent so delayed, duplicated, or out-of-order callbacks could not create duplicate activations or wrongful locks.'
definingDecision: 'Chose eventual consistency over real-time activation because a five-minute wait is less damaging than a wrongful disconnection caused by double-counted or delayed payments.'
outcome: 'DayLipa Finance launched in April 2023 and is operational across Davis & Shirtliff’s distribution network. 500+ solar pump devices are under active remote management, the USSD service application is used by field engineering teams, and the platform is targeting 112,000 PAYGO sales by 2029 with expansion planned into Uganda, Tanzania, and Zambia.'
