problem: 'Duka owners, many of them women-owned or women-operated, run on cash, travel physically to wholesalers, and have no access to formal credit. That limits inventory, increases stockouts, and keeps them trapped in an inefficient supply chain that has barely changed in decades.'
solution: 'Managed end-to-end delivery and pilot testing across 51 retailers in Kambu and Nairobi. Prioritised automated tracking and reporting over feature expansion during the pilot, replacing manual processes that consumed hours each week with data systems that fed funder and management reporting.'
definingDecision: 'Put data discipline before product expansion because the team needed clean pilot evidence to prove viability to funders before scaling the feature set.'
outcome: 'Pilot participants saw a 15% sales uplift, with shop owners ordering more frequently and increasing basket sizes because of credit access and delivery convenience. 51 retailers were onboarded across two regions, and automated reporting enabled the scale-up business case for digitised last-mile FMCG distribution with embedded micro-credit.'
