product: 'A Business Development Services mentorship platform for the Institute of Certified SIYB Trainers Kenya. It connects certified trainers with aspiring entrepreneurs through structured mentorship programmes, group facilitation sessions, and trainee progress tracking, digitising a process that had been informal and undocumented.'
problem: 'SIYB-certified trainers in Kenya had no digital infrastructure for mentorship: relationships lived in WhatsApp, trainee progress was untracked, and group sessions had no standard format. ICST had no data on whether mentorship was working, who was excelling, or how to scale what worked.'
solution: 'Led an 18+ session collaborative design and specification process with ICST stakeholders. Prioritised Group Sessions as a first-class product entity rather than treating them as calendar add-ons, then produced UX documentation aligned to 14 global HCD standards plus a 107-test-case UAT runbook with Playwright automation guidance.'
definingDecision: 'Designed group facilitation as the primary interaction model because trainer interviews showed group sessions, not only 1:1 mentorship, were where the most transformative learning happened.'
outcome: 'The complete specification suite is ready for development, including 107 test cases from trainer onboarding through group facilitation to trainee progress reporting. The HCD-compliant design system references 14 named global standards, including ISO 9241-210, WCAG 2.1 AA, Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics, and Gestalt principles.'
